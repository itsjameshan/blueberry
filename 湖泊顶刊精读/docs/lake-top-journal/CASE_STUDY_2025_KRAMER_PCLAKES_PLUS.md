# Case study: Kramer et al. (2025) — PCLakeS+

Paper: Modeling the impact of flushing on a lake meta-ecosystem using PCLakeS+.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolmodel.2025.111264
Full text: https://edepot.wur.nl/698808
Model files: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.148234034

## Writing lesson
The abstract progresses from the network-scale management problem, to model and aims, to qualified model performance, flow-data difficulty, limited response to flushing, downstream nutrient transport, and the need for additional measures. Learn from the balanced structure: include uncertainty, spatial exceptions, and management trade-offs rather than listing only positive findings.

## Figure lesson
For the network connectivity/residence-time figure, the scientific purpose is to connect network topology to transport and water residence. For model-evaluation figures, compare observations and simulations at representative upstream and downstream lakes, and make mismatches visible. For baseline/scenario figures, label 2013 versus 2020+ carefully: 2020+ is a long-term model scenario, not a field observation. Exact visual encodings must be checked directly against the PDF before replication.
