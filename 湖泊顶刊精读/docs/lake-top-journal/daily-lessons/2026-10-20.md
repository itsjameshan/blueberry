## 图形复现方法
- PCA morphospace: axis loadings, explained variance, category hull/density and scenario overlays.
- Predicted vs observed: 1:1 diagonal, MAE/RMSE, independent holdout, n, units.
- Interaction surface: 2D response or faceted curves; show uncertainty and avoid causal language for SHAP.
- Null-model simulations: compare observed statistics with simulated distribution and state iteration count.
